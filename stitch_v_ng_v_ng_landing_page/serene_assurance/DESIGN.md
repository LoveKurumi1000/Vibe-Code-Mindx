## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a serene, grounding, and empathetic environment tailored for university students and early-career graduates navigating high-stakes interview anxiety. The interface counterbalances systemic career stress with an atmosphere of composure, quiet authority, and validation.

The design movement embraces **Warm Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Generous Canvas Breathability**: Expansive whitespace prevents cognitive overload, giving nervous users visual and mental room to pause.
- **Humanistic Authority**: Classic editorial typography paired with modern, clean text sets a tone that is mentorship-driven rather than clinical or transactional.
- **Restraint Over Distraction**: Absence of high-velocity gradients, hyperactive micro-interactions, or neon accents. Depth is established through structural balance, subtle lines, and soft atmospheric layers.

## Colors

The palette leverages psychological anchors of stability, clarity, and gentle focus:

- **Primary Navy (`#1E3A5F`)**: Anchors critical messaging, editorial headlines, structural framing, and high-impact hero zones. Communicates reliability, deep preparation, and composure.
- **Deep Navy (`#16304F`)**: Used selectively for grounding elements such as structural footers, high-contrast modal backdrops, and immersive focus areas.
- **Action Blue (`#2F6FB0`)**: Designated strictly for interactive affordances, primary buttons, text links, and focused active states. Delivers an encouraging, positive call-to-action without aggressive urgency.
- **Light Calm Blue (`#E8EEF5`)**: Acts as a soothing tonal bed for highlight callouts, meta badges, icon backgrounds, and gentle interactive hover fills.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`)**: The bedrock for cards, interactive sheets, inputs, and distinct foreground content blocks.
- **Soft Background (`#FAFBFC`)**: The default canvas base, muting harsh screen glare and reducing visual fatigue.
- **Body Text (`#1F2933`)**: High-contrast, warm dark slate ensuring crisp legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary Muted (`#52606D`)**: Applied to supporting metadata, subtitles, form field hints, and secondary descriptions.
- **Border Stroke (`#E2E8F0`)**: A whisper-light perimeter definition for structural clarity.

## Typography

The typography pairs the warmth and dignity of **Lora** with the precision and diacritic clarity of **Be Vietnam Pro**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Lora)**: Commands headings with intellectual composure, validating the user’s self-worth and creating an authentic feeling of a distinguished mentorship publication.
- **Contemporary Sans (Be Vietnam Pro)**: Formulated with natural proportions and superior handling of complex tone marks, serving all body copy, interaction states, and operational data. It preserves readability during heightened anxiety.
- **Rhythm & Line Heights**: Reading comfort is prioritized via generous line heights (1.6x to 1.8x for paragraphs), preventing visual crowding and aiding comprehension under cognitive stress.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system is deployed on desktop, adapting to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Breakpoint Thresholds**: Mobile (`< 768px`), Tablet (`768px – 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`). Max content constraint is set to `1200px` to maintain optimal text measure and eye-sweep ergonomics.
- **Whitespace Allocation**: Section blocks utilize expansive vertical separation (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to deliberately slow the user’s pacing and encourage measured reading.
- **Component Padding**: Interactive elements honor strict interior padding rules—ensuring buttons and cards never choke their internal typography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Calm & Low-Contrast Borders**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas)**: Background `#FAFBFC`. Unbroken, calm, expansive.
- **Layer 1 (Card / Container)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-fine perimeter line `1px solid #E2E8F0`. 
- **Layer 1 Elevated (Hover / Interactive Float)**: Maintains the `1px solid #E2E8F0` stroke while introducing an ambient, deeply-diffused tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 2 (Overlays / Dialogs / Floating Menus)**: `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, with `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(22, 48, 79, 0.08)`.
- **Soft Inset Tones**: Inner badge fills and module groupings use `#E8EEF5` without any drop shadow, achieving hierarchy purely through soft contrast.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette conveys warmth, stability, and safety through balanced, gentle curvature:

- **Cards and Major Containers**: Uniformly finished with a radius of `0.875rem` to `1rem` (14px–16px), delivering an approachable, non-intrusive container.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs)**: Fixed at `0.75rem` (12px), creating a tactile, reassuring touchpoint.
- **Pills, Badges, and Micro-Tags**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) to create natural visual contrast against structural content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Action Blue (`#2F6FB0`) fill with White (`#FFFFFF`) text. Height of `48px`, `12px` border radius, horizontal padding `24px`. Subtle hover state shifts to a deeper blue (`#24588D`) with a gentle `0 4px 12px rgba(47, 111, 176, 0.16)` shadow.
- **Secondary**: Light Calm Blue (`#E8EEF5`) background with Primary Navy (`#1E3A5F`) text. Hover state darkens slightly to `#DCE5F0`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent background, Primary Navy text, with an underline appearing solely on focus/hover.

### Badges & Filter Chips
- **Status & Focus Badges**: Pill-shaped (`9999px`), Light Calm Blue (`#E8EEF5`) background, Primary Navy (`#1E3A5F`) text, typography set to `label-sm`. Used for category indicators such as "Anxiety Relief", "Mock Interview", and "Mindset Prep".

### Cards
- **Editorial Cards**: Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) background, `16px` border-radius, bounded by a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Padding scaled to `32px` on desktop and `20px` on mobile.
- **Testimonial & Reassurance Cards**: Tinted card variant using `#F4F7FA` surface with an accent left-border in Primary Navy (`#1E3A5F`, 3px width) to anchor authentic quotes and reflective advice.

### Input Fields
- **Text Inputs**: Height of `48px`, `12px` border-radius, Surface White background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `16px` internal padding. 
- **Focus State**: `1px solid #2F6FB0` combined with a soft, non-invasive ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(47, 111, 176, 0.12)`.
- **Labels & Hints**: Labels in `14px` Medium (`#1E3A5F`), helper hints in Secondary Muted (`#52606D`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Style**: `20px` dimensions, `6px` border-radius for checkboxes, circular for radios. Default state has a `1.5px` border in `#CBD5E1`. Selected state fills with Action Blue (`#2F6FB0`) with a white inner glyph.

### Specialized Component: Calming Reflection Accordion
- Structured for mental preparation FAQs and de-escalation tips. Clean horizontal rules `#E2E8F0` with Lora headings (`headline-sm`). Toggles reveal comfortably spaced text (`body-md`) with zero abrupt accordion snaps—utilizing smooth 250ms ease-out transitions.
